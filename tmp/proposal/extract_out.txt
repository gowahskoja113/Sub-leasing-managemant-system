45: a. Context:
46: The project focuses on developing a comprehensive management system for the business model of renting entire houses, renovating them, and subdividing them into smaller rooms for subleasing. This is a 
47: The system includes a mobile application for tenants and on-site operations managers, along with a centralized web-based admin portal to monitor cash flow, investment costs, and the overall operationa
48: Functional requirement :
49: Objectives: Based on the user requirement, the system is implemented as follows
50: 1.Build a Mobile Application: Enable tenants to view invoices, make payments, and submit maintenance requests easily.
51: 2.Build an Operations Management Application: Support managers in onboarding tenants, recording utility meter readings, and handling operational issues.
52: 3.Develop an Admin Dashboard: Provide administrators with real-time monitoring of revenue, expenses, and system performance.
53: 4.Implement an Automated Billing System: Automatically calculate and generate monthly invoices based on rental and utility data.
54: 5.Integrate a Payment System: Support QR Code and bank transfer payments with automatic status updates via webhook.
55: 6.Build a Maintenance Management System: Allow tenants to submit requests and enable managers to track and resolve issues efficiently.
56: 7.Two-way cash flow management: Automatically track and reconcile the rental cost of entire property (expenses) with income collected from individual rooms (revenue) to accurately calculate net profit
57: Non-functional requirement:
58: -Limitations:
59: oPayment integration is restricted to popular local methods (VietQR, bank transfers). International credit cards are not supported within the project scope.
60: oThe application requires a stable internet connection for push notifications, and data synchronization; offline functionality is not supported.
61: oUtility meter image recognition relies on an external OCR library; accuracy is highly dependent on the quality of the captured photos.
62: -Exclusions:
63: oHuman Resource Management: The system does not include HR functionalities such as payroll, attendance tracking, or employee performance evaluations.
64: oLegal & Contract Compliance: While the system generates electronic contracts based on templates, it does not guarantee comprehensive legal consulting or full legal validity.
65: oProperty Valuation: The system does not provide real estate valuation features or rental market analytics.

113: -As a tenant, I want to view my current room details, rental contract terms, and payment history.
114: -As a tenant, I want to receive push notifications when a new invoice is generated each month.
115: -As a tenant, I want to track the status of my maintenance requests from submission to resolution.
116: -As an Operations Manager, I want to board a new tenant by registering their information, capturing room condition photos, and recording initial utility meter readings.
117: -As an Operations Manager, I want to generate a digital rental contract and send it to the tenant for confirmation via OTP.
118: -As a System Admin, I want to manage the full property portfolio, including adding new houses, configuring room layouts, and setting rental pricing.
119: -As the System Handler, I want to automatically update room status (Available / Occupied / Maintenance) based on contract events.
120: Monthly Billing & Payment Workflow
121: -As an Operations Manager, I want to record end-of-month electricity and water meter readings (manually or by photo) for each room.
122: -As the System Handler, I want to automatically calculate monthly invoices including rent, electricity, water, service fees, and any outstanding balance
123: -As the System Handler, I want to push invoice notifications to tenants' mobile apps immediately after calculation.
124: -As a User, I want to view my itemized monthly invoice and pay via QR Code or bank transfer within the app.
125: -As the System Handler, I want to process payment webhooks from integrated banking services and automatically update invoice status to 'Paid'.
126: -As a System Admin, I want to view a real-time cash flow dashboard showing total revenue collected vs. total expenses for each property.
127: - As the System Handler, I want to automatically send payment reminders to tenants who have not paid by the due date.
128: Maintenance & Equipment Management Workflow
129: -As a User, I want to submit a maintenance request with a description, category (electrical, plumbing, furniture, etc.), and supporting photos.
130: -As an Operations Manager, I want to receive instant notifications for new maintenance requests and view them as priority.
131: -As an Operations Manager, I want to update the status of a maintenance request (Pending / In Progress / Resolved) and record repair costs.
132: -As the System Handler, I want to update the maintenance history for related equipment items when a repair is completed.
133: -As the System Handler, I want to send a resolution notification to the tenant when their request is resolved.
134: -As an Operations Manager, I want to manage the equipment inventory for each room, tracking item details from installation through the operational lifecycle.
135: Cash Flow & Financial Reporting Workflow
136: -As a System Admin, I want to record large-house lease expenses (monthly rent paid to property owners) to track the primary cost center.
137: -As a System Admin, I want to see a net profit/loss report per property, comparing room revenue against all associated expenses.
138: -As the System Handler, I want to automatically reconcile incoming payments against outstanding invoices and generate monthly financial summaries.
139: -As a System Admin, I want to export financial reports (revenue, expenses, profit) to Excel for external accounting.
