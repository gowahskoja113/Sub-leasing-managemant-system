(*) 3.1. Capstone Project name:
English: Sub-leasing Management System
Vietnamese: Hệ thống quản lý kinh doanh thuê và cho thuê lại bất động sản
a. Context:
The project focuses on developing a comprehensive management system for the business model of renting entire houses, renovating them, and subdividing them into smaller rooms for subleasing. This is a common business model in major cities in Vietnam; however, there is currently no specialized software solution to support efficient management.
The system includes a mobile application for tenants and on-site operations managers, along with a centralized web-based admin portal to monitor cash flow, investment costs, and the overall operational performance of the property portfolio. It addresses three core challenges: managing two-way cash flow (paying large rent for whole properties while collecting smaller rents from individual rooms), managing equipment from initial investment through operation, and automating rent collection and issue resolution processes.
Functional requirement :
Objectives: Based on the user requirement, the system is implemented as follows
1.Build a Mobile Application: Enable tenants to view invoices, make payments, and submit maintenance requests easily.
2.Build an Operations Management Application: Support managers in onboarding tenants, recording utility meter readings, and handling operational issues.
3.Develop an Admin Dashboard: Provide administrators with real-time monitoring of revenue, expenses, and system performance.
4.Implement an Automated Billing System: Automatically calculate and generate monthly invoices based on rental and utility data.
5.Integrate a Payment System: Support QR Code and bank transfer payments with automatic status updates via webhook.
6.Build a Maintenance Management System: Allow tenants to submit requests and enable managers to track and resolve issues efficiently.
7.Two-way cash flow management: Automatically track and reconcile the rental cost of entire property (expenses) with income collected from individual rooms (revenue) to accurately calculate net profit.
Non-functional requirement:
-Limitations:
oPayment integration is restricted to popular local methods (VietQR, bank transfers). International credit cards are not supported within the project scope.
oThe application requires a stable internet connection for push notifications, and data synchronization; offline functionality is not supported.
oUtility meter image recognition relies on an external OCR library; accuracy is highly dependent on the quality of the captured photos.
-Exclusions:
oHuman Resource Management: The system does not include HR functionalities such as payroll, attendance tracking, or employee performance evaluations.
oLegal & Contract Compliance: While the system generates electronic contracts based on templates, it does not guarantee comprehensive legal consulting or full legal validity.
oProperty Valuation: The system does not provide real estate valuation features or rental market analytics.
-Assumptions:
○Property data (houses, rooms, equipment) is initially inputted by System Admin and is maintained regularly.
○The business operators already hold valid master lease agreements with the original property owners before utilizing the system.
○Users (tenants and managers) possess smartphones with reliable internet access.
○Payment integration (banking webhooks/VietQR) functions reliably according to the partner's API documentation.
-Business rules:
○Billing Cycle: Invoices are automatically generated on a designated date each month after the manager records the electricity and water meter readings. Invoices cannot be created if the meter readings have not been recorded.
○Payment Deadline: Tenants must pay within the deadline specified in the lease contract. Late payments will incur additional fees and trigger automatic reminders.
○Security Deposit: The security deposit is recorded separately and will only be refunded upon contract termination, provided there is no property damage.
○Occupancy Rule: A room can only have one active lease contract at any given time. A new contract can only be created after the previous one has ended.

Products (Expected Deliverables):
Build a system that supports main features written as user stories as follows:
Tenant & Contract Management Workflow
-As a tenant, I want to register or log in to an account so that I can access billing and maintenance features.
-As a tenant, I want to view my current room details, rental contract terms, and payment history.
-As a tenant, I want to receive push notifications when a new invoice is generated each month.
-As a tenant, I want to track the status of my maintenance requests from submission to resolution.
-As an Operations Manager, I want to board a new tenant by registering their information, capturing room condition photos, and recording initial utility meter readings.
-As an Operations Manager, I want to generate a digital rental contract and send it to the tenant for confirmation via OTP.
-As a System Admin, I want to manage the full property portfolio, including adding new houses, configuring room layouts, and setting rental pricing.
-As the System Handler, I want to automatically update room status (Available / Occupied / Maintenance) based on contract events.
Monthly Billing & Payment Workflow
-As an Operations Manager, I want to record end-of-month electricity and water meter readings (manually or by photo) for each room.
-As the System Handler, I want to automatically calculate monthly invoices including rent, electricity, water, service fees, and any outstanding balance
-As the System Handler, I want to push invoice notifications to tenants' mobile apps immediately after calculation.
-As a User, I want to view my itemized monthly invoice and pay via QR Code or bank transfer within the app.
-As the System Handler, I want to process payment webhooks from integrated banking services and automatically update invoice status to 'Paid'.
-As a System Admin, I want to view a real-time cash flow dashboard showing total revenue collected vs. total expenses for each property.
- As the System Handler, I want to automatically send payment reminders to tenants who have not paid by the due date.
Maintenance & Equipment Management Workflow
-As a User, I want to submit a maintenance request with a description, category (electrical, plumbing, furniture, etc.), and supporting photos.
-As an Operations Manager, I want to receive instant notifications for new maintenance requests and view them as priority.
-As an Operations Manager, I want to update the status of a maintenance request (Pending / In Progress / Resolved) and record repair costs.
-As the System Handler, I want to update the maintenance history for related equipment items when a repair is completed.
-As the System Handler, I want to send a resolution notification to the tenant when their request is resolved.
-As an Operations Manager, I want to manage the equipment inventory for each room, tracking item details from installation through the operational lifecycle.
Cash Flow & Financial Reporting Workflow
-As a System Admin, I want to record large-house lease expenses (monthly rent paid to property owners) to track the primary cost center.
-As a System Admin, I want to see a net profit/loss report per property, comparing room revenue against all associated expenses.
-As the System Handler, I want to automatically reconcile incoming payments against outstanding invoices and generate monthly financial summaries.
-As a System Admin, I want to export financial reports (revenue, expenses, profit) to Excel for external accounting.
